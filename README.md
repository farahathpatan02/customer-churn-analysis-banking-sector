# 📊 Bank Customer Churn Analysis Using Data Analytics

## Overview

Customer retention is a critical challenge in the banking industry. This project analyzes customer churn patterns using data analytics and exploratory data analysis (EDA) techniques to identify the factors that influence customer attrition and recommend effective retention strategies.

The analysis focuses on customer demographics, financial behavior, account activity, and product usage to uncover actionable business insights that can help banks reduce churn and improve customer loyalty.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer churn distribution.
- Identify demographic factors affecting churn.
- Examine behavioral indicators linked to customer attrition.
- Evaluate financial variables influencing churn.
- Develop data-driven customer retention strategies.
- Generate business insights for decision-making.

## 📈 Key Findings

### Customer Churn Rate
- Approximately 20% of customers exited the bank.

### Geographic Impact
- Customers from Germany exhibited the highest churn rate compared to France and Spain.

### Age Factor
- Customers aged 40+ showed a significantly higher likelihood of churn.

### Customer Activity
- Inactive customers were substantially more likely to leave the bank.

### Product Usage
- Customers with fewer banking products demonstrated higher churn probability.

### Financial Insights
- Higher account balances did not necessarily guarantee customer retention, highlighting the need for targeted engagement strategies.
